FAQ

Freight, customs and delivery

What does a freight forwarder do in Namibia?

A freight forwarder arranges transport, documentation, customs clearance and delivery of goods on your behalf. SI ALL Logistics coordinates sea, air and road freight through one team.

Do you ship through Walvis Bay?

Yes. We coordinate container and break-bulk shipments through Walvis Bay, including port handling, customs clearance and onward delivery.

Do you handle customs clearance in Namibia?

Yes. We prepare and submit import and export declarations and coordinate clearance at ports, airports and border posts.

Do you offer cross-border trucking?

Yes. We coordinate cross-border road freight on the main regional corridors, including border paperwork.

Do you offer warehousing in Windhoek?

Yes. We offer short-term warehousing, sorting, consolidation and last-mile delivery in Windhoek.

What do I need to send to get a quote?

Cargo type, weight and dimensions, the origin and destination, the delivery date you need, and the invoice and packing list if you have them.

Are your quotes in Namibian Dollars?

Yes. Quotes are itemised in Namibian Dollars (N$), with clear deposit and balance terms.
