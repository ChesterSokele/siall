Our story

Logistics at the core, trade around it.

We started with a simple idea: moving goods should be reliable, transparent and personal. Logistics is our core business, and our trading divisions in sports apparel and food & beverages grow out of the same strengths: sourcing, production oversight and delivery.

Based in Olympia, Windhoek, we serve businesses, clubs and institutions across Namibia and the region.
