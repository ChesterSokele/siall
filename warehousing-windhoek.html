Overview

Warehousing by Namibia's #1 logistics company.

Warehousing gives your cargo a safe place to wait between arrival and delivery, or to be sorted, consolidated and split into smaller deliveries. It is especially useful for importers who receive large shipments and distribute them in stages.

Because SI ALL Logistics handles the freight and the delivery too, your goods move from the port or airport into storage and out to customers without handovers between companies.

  • Short-term storage and sorting
  • Consolidation of multiple suppliers
  • Split deliveries and last-mile distribution
  • Linked to freight and customs

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer warehousing in Windhoek?

Yes. SI ALL Logistics offers short-term warehousing, sorting and consolidation in Windhoek, together with last-mile delivery.

Can you consolidate shipments from different suppliers?

Yes. We can combine cargo from several suppliers and deliver it as a single shipment, or split a large shipment into several deliveries.

Is storage linked to customs clearance?

Yes. Because we also handle freight and clearance, your goods can move from the port or airport into storage with the paperwork already in order.

Do you deliver from the warehouse?

Yes. Last-mile delivery to your customers, shops or sites is part of the service.
