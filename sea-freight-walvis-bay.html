Overview

Sea freight by Namibia's #1 logistics company.

Walvis Bay is Namibia's main commercial port and the gateway for sea cargo to Namibia and onward into the region. Sea freight is the most economical way to move heavy, bulky and non-urgent cargo.

SI ALL Logistics books the space, prepares the shipping documents, coordinates port handling and customs clearance, and arranges the road leg from Walvis Bay to Windhoek or to your final destination.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you ship through Walvis Bay?

Yes. SI ALL Logistics coordinates container and break-bulk shipments through Walvis Bay, including port handling, customs clearance and onward delivery.

What is the difference between FCL and LCL?

FCL (full container load) means you use a whole container for your cargo. LCL (less than container load) means your goods share a container with others, which suits smaller shipments.

When should I choose sea freight over air freight?

Choose sea freight for heavy, bulky or non-urgent cargo where cost matters most. Choose air freight when speed matters more than cost.

Can you deliver from Walvis Bay to Windhoek?

Yes. We arrange road transport from Walvis Bay to Windhoek and other destinations as part of the same shipment.
