What makes a logistics company the right choice in Namibia?

Logistics goes wrong in the handovers between carriers, customs and warehouses. When choosing a provider in Namibia, look for:

  • One point of contact from booking to delivery
  • Customs expertise so declarations are right the first time
  • Access to every mode: sea via Walvis Bay, air via Windhoek and road corridors
  • Clear, itemised pricing in Namibian Dollars
  • Local presence and a real person you can phone or WhatsApp

That is how SI ALL Logistics works, and it is why we aim to be the first company Namibian businesses think of for logistics.
