Overview

Air freight by Namibia's #1 logistics company.

Air freight is the fastest way to move goods, and the right choice for urgent, perishable or high-value cargo. In Namibia, international air cargo is handled through Hosea Kutako International Airport near Windhoek.

SI ALL Logistics arranges the collection, air booking, documents, customs clearance and the final delivery, so a single team is accountable for the whole journey.

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Do you offer air freight in Namibia?

Yes. SI ALL Logistics arranges air freight for urgent and high-value cargo to and from Windhoek, including collection, customs clearance and final delivery.

When is air freight worth the cost?

Air freight is worth it when speed matters: urgent spare parts, perishable goods, high-value items or tight deadlines.

Can you collect the cargo from my supplier?

Yes. We can arrange collection and delivery to the airport as part of the shipment.

What do I need to send for an air freight quote?

Cargo type, weight and dimensions, origin and destination, and the date you need it delivered.
