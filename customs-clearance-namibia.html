Overview

Customs clearance by Namibia's #1 logistics company.

Customs clearance is the process of declaring imported or exported goods to the customs authority, paying any duties and taxes due, and getting the goods released. In Namibia, customs is administered by the Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA).

Mistakes on a declaration cause delays, storage costs and penalties. SI ALL Logistics prepares and submits the documentation for you, and keeps you informed at each step, whether your cargo arrives by sea at Walvis Bay, by air at Windhoek or by road at a border post.

  • Import and export declarations prepared accurately
  • Permits and supporting documents checked before you ship
  • Duty and tax paperwork explained, no surprises
  • Clearance at ports, airports and border posts

FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What documents are needed for customs clearance in Namibia?

Typically a commercial invoice, packing list and a bill of lading or air waybill, plus any import permits or certificates the goods require. SI ALL Logistics checks your documents before shipping so clearance is not held up.

Who handles customs in Namibia?

Customs in Namibia is administered by the Namibia Revenue Agency (NamRA). A clearing agent such as SI ALL Logistics prepares and submits declarations on your behalf.

Can you clear goods at Walvis Bay?

Yes. We coordinate customs clearance for sea cargo arriving at Walvis Bay, as well as air cargo at Windhoek and road cargo at border posts.

How long does customs clearance take?

It depends on the goods, the completeness of the documents and any inspections. Accurate paperwork is the biggest factor, which is why we check documents before shipment.
